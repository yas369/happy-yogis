    What the pattern says

      Your daily limit

      Estimated from your body and activity with the Mifflin–St Jeor formula. It is an estimate, not a prescription: if your weight doesn't move after 3–4 weeks, adjust it.

      Reminders

      A reminder is skipped if you have already logged that meal.

      Get the reminders on your phone

      Most reliable: add them to your calendar. Your phone's calendar alerts you even when this app is closed, on iPhone and Android.

      iPhone: open the file and tap Add All. Android: Google Calendar can't open .ics files, so use the links below. Each one adds a daily repeating event. In Google Calendar, set its notification to "at time of event".

      App notifications

      These fire while the app is open or recently used. On Android, if you install the app to your home screen, Chrome may also check in the background, but it decides when, so treat that as a bonus. On iPhone, notifications only work after you Add to Home Screen from Safari's share menu.
