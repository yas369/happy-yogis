About the centre

A neighbourhood centre, not a fitness chain

Happy Yogis runs out of a hall in SSM Nagar, New Perungalathur. On any given morning you will find school children, people on their way to work, homemakers and retired members on the mats at the same time. That mix is deliberate — it is what keeps the room relaxed.

Classes are small enough that the teacher knows what each person is working with: a bad knee, a desk job, a thyroid condition, a first week of ever having tried yoga. The practice gets adjusted around that rather than the other way round.

We are not interested in whether you can put your leg behind your head. We care that you turn up, breathe properly, and leave feeling better than when you walked in.

What we teach

Classes at Happy Yogis

Everything below is taught within the regular batches — you do not book a separate course. Tell us what you are working towards and the teacher builds it into your practice.

Beginner yoga

If you have never held a pose before, start here. We build the standing postures and the breath slowly, and nobody is pushed past what their body will do that day.

Weight loss and stamina

A faster-moving practice built around sun salutations and standing sequences, for students whose main goal is losing weight and building stamina.

Flexibility and posture

Hip openers, hamstring work and spinal movement. Popular with students who sit at a desk all day and arrive with a stiff neck and lower back.

Meditation and pranayama

Breathing practice and seated meditation. Every batch ends with this, and some students come specifically for it.

Yoga for women

Practice adapted around PCOD, thyroid and hormonal health, and around the menstrual cycle. The 10:00 am batch is ladies only.

Yoga for children

Children join the morning and evening batches with their parents. The focus is on balance, concentration and making it enjoyable rather than perfect.

Yoga for seniors

Low-impact practice with chair and wall support where it is needed, for joint mobility and steadiness.

Corporate and group sessions

Sessions for offices and residential associations, at your premises or online. Ask us for details.

Health-focused practice

Practising around a health condition

A good number of our students come to yoga because a doctor suggested it. Tell us what you are dealing with before your first class so the teacher can plan around it.

Yoga supports treatment; it does not replace it. If you are under medical care, please keep your doctor in the loop.

Your teacher

Karthika

Karthika has been practising and teaching yoga for more than ten years, and teaches every batch at the centre herself.

Her approach is to meet people where they are. Beginners are not pushed into postures they are not ready for, and students working around an injury or a health condition get the practice adapted rather than being told to sit it out.

Where to find us

One centre, in New Perungalathur

Happy Yogis has a single centre — in SSM Nagar, New Perungalathur, just off the GST Road stretch between Tambaram and Vandalur, close to Perungalathur railway station. There are no other branches. Students travel in from the neighbourhoods nearby, and we also teach online for anyone who cannot make it in person.
