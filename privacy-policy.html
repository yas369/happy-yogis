Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

At Happy Yogis Yoga Centre, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or use our services.

Effective Date: June 2026

01 Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and WhatsApp contact details when you inquire about classes or book sessions.
  • Health Information: Medical conditions or physical limitations you voluntarily share with our instructors for safe practice guidance.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited and time spent.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and booking requests via WhatsApp or phone.
  • Schedule and manage your yoga class appointments.
  • Send class reminders, schedule updates, and wellness tips (with your consent).
  • Improve our website experience and class offerings.
  • Ensure your safety during yoga practice by understanding any health conditions.

03 How We Protect Your Data

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information:

  • Your contact information is stored securely and accessed only by authorized staff.
  • We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • Health information shared with instructors is kept confidential and used solely for your safety.
  • We use secure communication channels for all inquiries and bookings.

04 Third-Party Services

Our website uses third-party services to enhance your experience:

  • WhatsApp: We use WhatsApp for class bookings and communication. Your messages are subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps: Our location map is powered by Google Maps, subject to Google's privacy practices.
  • Social Media: Links to Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube are subject to their respective privacy policies.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party platforms. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

05 Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use essential cookies to ensure proper functionality. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising or analytics purposes. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

06 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (where applicable).
  • Opt out of promotional communications at any time.

07 Children's Privacy

We offer kids yoga classes with parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

08 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
