Yoga classes in Perungalathur

Happy Yogis is a yoga centre in SSM Nagar, New Perungalathur, and this is our only location — so if you live in Perungalathur, this is your local class rather than a drive across the city.

Book a 3-day trial +91 99942 47450

Where we are

The centre is in SSM Nagar, New Perungalathur, close to Perungalathur railway station and just off the GST Road stretch running down towards Vandalur. Students walk or ride in from New Perungalathur, Old Perungalathur and the surrounding streets.

Who practises here

The batches are genuinely mixed — school children before class, people fitting a session in before the commute, women in the mid-morning ladies batch, and retired members who come for the joint mobility and the company. Most people who join have never done yoga before.

What a class looks like

Every batch runs warm-up, asanas, pranayama and a closing relaxation. Sessions are 45 or 60 minutes. If you are working around a knee problem, back pain, PCOD or a thyroid condition, tell the teacher beforehand and the practice is adjusted for you.

Getting started

Message us on WhatsApp and we will book you a 3-day trial in whichever batch has room. Bring a mat if you have one; once you join you can leave it at the centre.
