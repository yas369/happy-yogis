Yoga classes near Tambaram

Happy Yogis is in New Perungalathur, the next stop down the GST Road and the suburban line from Tambaram — close enough that a good number of our regulars travel in from East Tambaram, West Tambaram and Selaiyur.

Getting here from Tambaram

The centre is in SSM Nagar, New Perungalathur, near Perungalathur railway station — one stop along the Chengalpattu line from Tambaram, or a straight run south on the GST Road. Students from Selaiyur and East Tambaram usually come by two-wheeler.

Which batch suits a Tambaram commute

If you are heading into the city afterwards, the 5:30 am and 6:15 am batches work best. The 5:00 pm evening batch is the popular one for school and college students travelling back from Tambaram. The 10:00 am batch is ladies only.

Or practise from home

We also teach online, which several Tambaram-side students use on days when the traffic on GST Road is not worth it. Same teacher, same batch structure.

What we teach

Beginner yoga, weight-loss and stamina work, flexibility and posture, pranayama and meditation, plus practice adapted around PCOD, thyroid, knee pain, back problems and pregnancy. Children practise alongside their parents.
